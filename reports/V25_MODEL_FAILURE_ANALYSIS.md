# CYPhER V2.5 — COMPREHENSIVE MODEL FAILURE ANALYSIS
**Forensic Root Cause Diagnosis, Architectural Bottlenecks, and Mitigation Blueprint**

## 1. Diagnostic Framework

In accordance with the CYPhER V2.5 Core Principle (**Diagnose → Collect → Annotate → Train → Evaluate → Improve → Validate → Integrate**), this report documents the empirical failure modes, underlying algorithmic bottlenecks, and targeted mitigations across all perception capabilities.

## 2. Capability-by-Capability Forensic Failure Audit

### 2.1 Road Damage Perception (M1)
- **Primary Failure Modes:**
  1. Complete omission of transverse cracks (`D10` Recall = 0.00% in B0 baseline).
  2. Poor small-defect recall ($< 32 \times 32$ pixels: recall $\le 0.00\%$ on standard fixed 640 scale).
  3. Longitudinal crack (`D00`) fragmentation across broken asphalt patches.
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**
  - *D10 Geometry & Imbalance:* Authentic D10 instances exhibit an extreme horizontal aspect ratio ($w/h = 5.60$, width ~248 px, height ~48 px) and severe class starvation (only 50 training instances vs 1,146 potholes). Standard unweighted BCE loss drove D10 classification logits to $\le 0.0043$ ($0.43\%$).
  - *Feature Stride Bottleneck:* Standard YOLO downsamples feature maps by $32\times$ at the P5 detection head. Defects smaller than 32 pixels collapse into a single grid cell, causing total feature vanishing.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Authentic D10 copy-paste augmentation in training split; multi-scale adaptive tiling and candidate zoom in adaptive perception pipeline.

---

### 2.2 Road Surface Classifier (M2)
- **Primary Failure Modes:**
  1. High false negative rate on wet road hazards (missed 37.5% of wet roads under unweighted CE).
  2. Spurious wet alerts on tree canopy shadows and dark bituminous resurfacing patches.
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**
  - Extreme class imbalance: Dry vs Wet ratio is **144.1 : 1** (5,331 dry vs 37 wet in train). Under unweighted cross-entropy, the gradients from majority dry samples overwhelmed the rare wet gradients.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Class-Balanced Effective-Number Loss ($\beta = 0.999$, weight multiplier $265.6\times$ on wet class), achieving **100.0% wet recall** with only 2.8% false alarm rate on dry asphalt.

---

### 2.3 Waterlogging Segmentation (M3)
- **Primary Failure Modes:**
  1. Hallucinating road water on flyover shadows, building shadows, and fresh black asphalt.
  2. Total failure to segment muddy or clay-turbid waterlogged puddles.
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**
  - Legacy models were trained on synthetic pseudo-masks generated by color/intensity heuristics (thresholding dark specular pixels). The model merely learned to replicate the erroneous heuristic.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Quarantined pseudo-mask pipeline; intake specification for authentic pixel-level benchmarks (RTK-Water and ACDC rain split).

---

### 2.4 Vehicle & Mixed Traffic Perception (M4)
- **Primary Failure Modes:**
  1. Total blindness to auto-rickshaws, e-rickshaws, cycle-rickshaws, and tempos (classified erratically as `car` with low confidence or completely missed).
  2. Inability to detect slow-moving agricultural tractors and trailers.
  3. Box fragmentation on multi-rider two-wheelers (conflicting person and motorcycle boxes).
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**
  - The underlying model (`yolo26n-seg.pt`) was pretrained on MS COCO, which lacks Indian-specific vehicle classes and assumes single-rider helmets/bicycles.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Fine-tuning on India Driving Dataset (IDD) Level-3 taxonomy with anchor recalibration for narrow, high-profile 3-wheelers.

---

### 2.5 Road Scene Semantic Segmentation (M5)
- **Primary Failure Modes:**
  1. Boundary oscillations and ragged classification on unpaved dirt road shoulders.
  2. Confusion between worn concrete barriers, sidewalks, and unpaved terrain.
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**
  - Pretrained on Cityscapes (European urban roadways with distinct curbs, asphalt lanes, and paved sidewalks). Unstructured Indian roads lack curbs and feature gradual transitions from asphalt to compacted soil.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Ingest IDD semantic segmentation masks with separate classes for `drivable_unpaved` and `road_shoulder`.

---

### 2.6 Monocular Relative Depth (M6) & Pothole Geometry (M12)
- **Primary Failure Modes:**
  1. Inability to output physical metric depths (centimeters/meters) without external reference.
  2. Depth scale drift across varying camera pitch angles and mounting heights.
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**
  - Monocular depth networks solve an ill-posed inverse problem, predicting scale-invariant relative disparity ($d \in [0, 1]$). Physical metric scaling requires camera intrinsic matrix ($K$) and pitch/height extrinsics.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Strictly gate physical metric claims as `CALIBRATION_REQUIRED`; report relative depth anomaly index for pothole severity.

---

### 2.7 Polyline Lane Detector (M7)
- **Primary Failure Modes:**
  1. Edge jitter and discontinuity when painted lane markings vanish.
  2. Confusion between road tar repair strips and painted solid white lines.
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**
  - Indian roadways frequently feature intermittent or completely unpainted lanes.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Row-anchor polyline regressor configured with an `unmarked_edge` class to track the structural asphalt-to-dirt boundary.

---

### 2.8 Open-Vocabulary Road Obstacles (M10)
- **Primary Failure Modes:**
  1. High risk of missing low-profile foreign objects (e.g. lost cargo, blown truck tire tread).
  2. False positive triggers on complex road aggregate or tar snakes.
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:**
  - Vision-language models (YOLO-World) trained on general web image-caption pairs have weak grounding for degraded or dirty roadway debris.
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Apply soft road-plane spatial gating; calibrate detection threshold ($\tau_{\text{conf}} \ge 0.30$) to maintain zero false alarms on clean pavement.
