## 1. Problem Statement: Why Heuristic Pseudo-Masks Failed

In CYPhER V2.0, the water segmenter (`runs/water_segmenter/best_water_model.pt`) was trained on pseudo-masks generated by color and reflection thresholding in OpenCV. The V2.1 audit demonstrated that this caused severe failure modes:
1. **Specular Glare False Positives:** Dry asphalt under direct sunlight generates intense specular highlights that the heuristic labeled as water puddles.
2. **Lack of Negative Boundary Constraints:** Pseudo-labels failed to distinguish damp road surface darkening from genuine accumulated standing water depth.
3. **Quarantine Mandate:** Per Engineering Rule 9, heuristic masks are strictly prohibited from authentic ground-truth training splits.

## 3. Ingestion Pipeline & Quality Assurance

The ingestion engine in `road_cv/water/authentic_water_dataset.py` enforces strict validation rules before any image-mask pair enters training:
1. **Cryptographic Duplicate & Leakage Check:** Calculates MD5 hashes for all images to ensure 0% train-to-val contamination.
2. **Mask-Image Dimension Alignment:** Rejects masks whose spatial dimensions $(H, W)$ do not match the RGB input frame.
3. **Class ID Integrity:** Verifies that mask pixel values belong exclusively to $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$:
   - `0`: Dry asphalt / concrete pavement
   - `1`: Standing water / flood
   - `2`: Localized water puddle
   - `3`: Specular wet reflection / glare
4. **Zero Pseudo-Mask Guarantee:** Refuses to synthesize masks on missing files, throwing an explicit `FileNotFoundError` rather than silently generating heuristic fallback masks.
